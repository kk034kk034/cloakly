Delete your Cloakly account

Operator: Cloud52. Address: Taiwan. Package id com.cloud52.cloakly. 繁體中文：刪除帳號. What is deleted and what remains: Privacy Policy.

Delete in the app

  1. Open Cloakly and sign in with the confirmed email and password.
  2. Open the account and subscription screen.
  3. Choose delete account. Confirm that you must cancel the store subscription separately, then enter the password.
  4. The app checks the password again and calls delete-account. The confirmation email must match the account email.

The cloud account cannot be restored. Meetings, transcripts, notes, the project index, and recordings stay on that phone until you delete the app or clear its storage. Cancel the App Store or Google Play subscription separately. The next period can still be charged, and deletion does not refund it.

If you cannot sign in

Email contact@cloud-52.com from the account email address with the subject "Delete Cloakly account" and include that email in the body. If you can still sign in, use the in-app steps above.
