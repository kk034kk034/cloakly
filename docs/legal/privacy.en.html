Cloakly mobile Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2026-10-26

This policy covers the Cloakly mobile apps on the App Store and Google Play (iOS and Android, package id com.cloud52.cloakly). The desktop app, when you supply your own API keys, sends data to the services you choose and is outside this policy.

Other language: 繁體中文. Terms: English. Account deletion: How to delete an account.

Who processes your data

Operator: Cloud52
Address: Taiwan
Email: contact@cloud-52.com

"We" means Cloud52. The backend is Supabase. Account and usage data are stored with our cloud provider Supabase, in data centers it operates. The project configuration in this repository does not record a region, so this policy does not name one.

What the product is, and consent to record

Cloakly is an on-screen meeting assistant. It is not a hidden window and it is not a tool for cheating on interviews or exams. The microphone is used only after you start a meeting.

Before you record, you must tell the other participants and get their agreement, and you must follow the law that applies to the conversation. We do not collect that consent for you and we do not check that you have it.

Free allowance as implemented

A free account can transcribe up to 1,800 seconds (30 minutes) per UTC day, plus 5 project questions and 5 plan suggestions. Cloakly Pro does not consume those daily caps. Every live transcription session, including Pro, still stops at 30 minutes. There is also a shared daily pool for all free accounts. That is an operations limit, not an advertising setting.

Third parties

These services process data to provide the product. Re-read their current terms before you submit the app, and update this page if they differ.

  • Supabase hosts auth, the database, and Edge Functions. Functions handle text in memory and return a result. They do not write the transcript into a table. On failure, the code may log the provider error body. Those logs are retained for a limited period. The number of days follows the Supabase plan and settings then in effect, and this policy does not state a specific number. Connection logs may include IP addresses. Our tables have no IP column, and the app does not turn IP addresses into a location.
  • Soniox (privacy policy, security notes) performs live transcription. The backend exchanges its long-lived key for a single-use key of about 60 seconds at api.soniox.com. The phone then opens the WebSocket. Public Soniox documents read on 2026-10-26 say real-time requests are processed transiently, customer content is not used to train models, and operational logs do not contain raw audio or transcripts, though usage records can include client_reference_id. Cloakly does not call the async API that stores audio files. If Soniox's latest terms differ, those terms win and this page should be updated.
  • OpenAI is called only when you use answer suggestions, minutes, project Q&A, or plan suggestions. The backend calls https://api.openai.com/v1/chat/completions. The default model is gpt-4o-mini, overridable with the server environment variable OPENAI_CHAT_MODEL. The payload is text, not audio. Cloakly does not send content to any other large language model. OpenAI's public API data controls say that data sent to the API is not used to train or improve OpenAI models by default unless the customer explicitly opts in, and that abuse-monitoring logs may include prompts and responses and are retained for up to 30 days for this endpoint, or longer when the law requires it or when that is reasonably necessary to prevent harm. This code does not send a training opt-in and does not request zero data retention. If OpenAI's policy or the project's settings differ, that current policy controls.
  • RevenueCat (privacy policy) handles purchases, restores, and entitlements. We set the app user id and the email. Account deletion tries to delete the RevenueCat subscriber. If the secret is missing or the call fails, the Supabase user is still deleted and the RevenueCat profile may remain.
  • Apple and Google distribute the app, take payment, manage subscriptions, and may show us crash reports you choose to share. They process the card number. We do not.

We do not sell personal information and we do not use this data for cross-app advertising.

Retention and deletion

  • Meetings, transcripts, notes, the project index, and recordings stay on the phone until you delete the app or clear its storage. In-app account deletion does not erase that on-device data.
  • Account deletion asks for your password again and calls delete-account. The function checks the email, tries to delete the RevenueCat subscriber, stores that day's free usage under an email hash, deletes webhook events whose payload contains your user id, then deletes the Supabase user. Account tables cascade from that user.
  • free_device_claims has no foreign key to the user. Account deletion does not remove those rows. What remains is the SHA-256 of the device id, the UTC date, and the id of the deleted user.
  • Email-hash rows for a past UTC date are deleted the next time any account is deleted or registered. The current day's row stays until a deletion or registration after that date.
  • Deleting the Cloakly account does not cancel or refund the store subscription. Cancel it in the App Store or Google Play.
  • Backups and logs at Soniox, OpenAI, RevenueCat, Supabase, and the stores are not deleted by a switch in this app. Deletion reaches only what the function above does.

Security

Connections to Supabase, Soniox, OpenAI, and RevenueCat use TLS (HTTPS or WSS). The on-device SQLite database is not encrypted by the app. Use a device passcode. We do not promise that transmission or storage cannot be compromised.

Children

The service is not directed at children. You must be at least 13 years old to create an account. We do not knowingly collect an account from someone under 13.

International transfers

Supabase, Soniox, OpenAI, RevenueCat, Apple, and Google may process data outside Taiwan and outside your country, in data centers those providers operate. This policy does not name a Supabase region, because the project configuration in this repository does not record one. Where the other services keep data follows their current documentation.

Your requests

You can delete the account in the app. For access, correction, or deletion when you cannot sign in, email contact@cloud-52.com from the account address. There is no transcript archive on the server to export; the transcript is on your phone. There is no separate web portal for privacy requests.

Changes

If the processing changes in a material way, we will update this page and the effective date. Continued use is of the version then published.
